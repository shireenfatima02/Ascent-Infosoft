LMS

An Intelligent Leave Management System

Simplified Approvals. Automated Tracking. Effortless Workforce Management.

SmartLeave LMS is a comprehensive leave management solution designed to automate time-off requests, streamline approvals, and maintain accurate attendance records. From small businesses to large enterprises, our system eliminates paperwork while ensuring compliance with company policies and labor regulations.

Key Features & Capabilities

Leave Request & Approval

  • Multi-level approval workflows (Manager/HR/Department Head).

  • Employee self-service portal for leave applications.

  • Real-time status notifications.

  • Mobile submission and approvals.

Attendance Integration

  • Automatic synchronization with biometric/RFID systems.

  • Holiday calendar management.

  • Shift pattern recognition.

  • Overtime calculation.

Policy Configuration

  • Customizable leave types (Paid, Sick, Maternity, etc.).

  • Accrual rules based on tenure/department/role.
