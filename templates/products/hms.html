HomoeoSoft

An All-in-One Homoeopathic Hospital Management System

Seamless Patient Care. Smarter Administration. Superior Outcomes.

HomoeoSoft is a comprehensive hospital management solution designed to digitize and streamline clinical and administrative workflows for hospitals, clinics, and health institutions. From patient registration to final discharge, our system ensures efficiency, accuracy, and compliance — all in one place.

Key Features & Capabilities

Centralized Patient Registration

  • Register new patients and access returning patients with ease.
  • Maintain complete patient histories in a single, searchable database.
  • Unique ID generation for lifetime record tracking.

Role-Based Access & Security

  • Permissions based on roles (Doctor, Nurse, Receptionist, Admin, etc.).
  • Secure sensitive data and patient records from unauthorized access.

Medicine Dispensing for OPD & IPD

  • Integrated pharmacy module for streamlined medicine issue.
  • Automated dispensing logs linked with patient treatment records.

Administrative Control Panel

  • Maintain and update departments, doctors, medicines, labs, and user roles.
  • Built-in analytics and automated reports for data-driven decision-making.

Diagnostics & Lab Integration

  • Prescribe pathological and radiological tests directly from the patient dashboard.
  • Attach, store, and view reports – accessible by both OPD and IPD departments.
  • Detailed investigation records maintained for each patient.

In Patient (IPD) Management

  • Manage IPD admissions, discharge summaries, and case papers.
  • Daily rounds, observations, treatment plans, and investigations – all logged digitally.
  • Track bed occupancy, status, and patient progress in real time.

Out Patient (OPD) Management

  • Effortless OPD entries with real-time data sync.
  • Digital OPD case papers accessible anytime.
  • Access and manage previous visit records, prescriptions, and history.

Homeopathy & CCRH/NABH Alignment

  • Built-in support for homeopathic case taking formats.
  • Structured as per CCRH and NABH guidelines.
  • Capture case data in a clinical, compliant, and standardized manner.

ICD-11 Disease Classification

  • Integrated WHO-specified ICD-11 encoding for accurate disease classification.
  • Facilitates global compliance and improved interoperability.

Special Registers

  • Diet chart logs, physiotherapy schedules, and yoga therapy records for OPD & IPD.
  • Easily assign treatments and monitor patient responses over time.

Fees & Billing Management

  • Track fees for:
  • Patient registration
  • OPD/IPD visits
  • Medicines
  • Diagnostics
  • Secure billing module with discounts, refunds, and dues tracking.
  • Generate invoices instantly and maintain transaction logs.

Reports & Analytics

  • Generate reports for:
  • Central Registration, OPD, IPD
  • Case Papers, Medicines, Investigations, IPD Occupancy
  • Fee collections and outstanding dues
  • Summary reports:
  • Day-wise, department-wise, gender-wise summary reports for both OPD and IPD
  • Export options in PDF/Excel formats.

Case Correction & Editing

  • Modify case details in case of data entry errors.
  • Admin-supervised correction logs for accountability and transparency.
